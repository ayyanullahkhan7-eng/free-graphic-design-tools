Free Graphic Design Tools for Students

Discover free graphic design tools for students. Create posters, presentations, social media graphics, and other creative projects using easy online tools.

1. Canva: Easy Online Design

Canva is an online design platform that offers templates for presentations, posters, and social media graphics. Students can use its available free features for educational projects.

Explore Canva

2. Adobe Express: Creative Designs

Adobe Express helps users create flyers, graphics, and other visual content. Students can explore its free features and design templates.

Explore Adobe Express

3. Photopea: Online Image Editing

Photopea is a browser-based image editor. It supports common image editing tasks and can be useful for students learning design.

Explore Photopea
